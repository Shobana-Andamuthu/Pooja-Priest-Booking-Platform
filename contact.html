Quick Help

Common Questions

How do I book a priest?

Choose a ritual, pick your date and preferred priest, then confirm. You receive a confirmation and your samagri list instantly.

Is the samagri included?

You can choose a package with full samagri delivered to your home, or request the checklist and arrange items yourself.

Can I reschedule a booking?

Yes. Bookings can be rescheduled free of charge up to 48 hours before the ritual from your dashboard.
